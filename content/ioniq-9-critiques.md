**Access**

- Should shutdown and lock when key walks away (Macan handles this perfectly)
- Similarly there is no need to have a start / stop button.
- Remote is huge, feels cheap, and has tons of small side buttons plus spare key doesn't store inside ← maybe the cheapest feeling, least well designed part of the car

**Drive**

- The car should maintain the mode it is set in and 1 pedal driving should be sticky. Changing modes on people is counterintuitive and dangerous
- Reverse should have a 1 pedal mode too
- The rear camera guidelines are not accurate. The car does not actually land where the curves are. 

**Storage**

- Rear trunk is slightly too small
- Door storages are way too small
- Cargo cover only works when 3rd row is down, should have a slot when up too

**Exterior**

- Exterior is mostly fine, I think the lights arcing over the back is not particularly attractive - maybe  if the lights had a more aggressive pattern in them. The 5 is much better looking. 
- Should have a charging port on each side and a space to store the adapter under the cover next to the port
- The popout door handles are chunky and unattractive and unnecessary and also reveal if you accidentally left your car unlocked

**Interior**

- The swoops and circles on the door panels are unattractive and generally not thematically fit with the exterior 
- The decorative passenger side panel looks like some kids started smudging silver paint with their fingers - it looks terrible. 
- The two tone is weird as hell
- The IR rejection of the car is terrible. They clearly cheaped out on glass. 
- I think they could have easily stretched this car vertically a couple more inches. A little more leg height and a little more airy cabin would have been great. 
- The rear seat should have a sunroof too. 

**Infotainment**

- I got this car instead of a Rivian because it has CarPlay and it is well executed
- The screen is nice and laid out nicely, but could have been 2 inches taller
- Some functions are in Blue Link but not on the car system which is weird (climate at time)
- The HUD is also slightly short and could have been wider and taller. 
- The digital mirror focal point should be like the HUD and set at distance
